Terms of use

The rules for using the yellowg PT training app. Last updated 24 September 2026.

What this is

yellowg PT is a private training app made by Juha Elo for a small number of coaching clients. It shows your training programme, records what you do, and lets your coach follow your progress. It is provided free of charge and is not a commercial product.

Training carries risk

The app is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Nothing in it diagnoses, treats or monitors a health condition.

Exercise involves a risk of injury. Before starting or substantially changing how you train, talk to a doctor — particularly if you have an existing condition, an injury, or symptoms such as chest pain, dizziness or shortness of breath. Stop training and seek medical attention if something feels wrong.

You decide what you actually do. A prescribed session is a suggestion from your coach, not an instruction to follow through pain. The app records what you did; it cannot see how you feel.

Wearable readings — sleep, recovery, heart rate and the rest — are consumer-grade estimates from Oura or Polar. Treat them as rough signals, not measurements to act on medically.

Your account

  • Accounts are created by your coach for you personally. Do not share your sign-in with anyone.
  • The sign-in code arrives by email and is valid briefly. Requesting a new one cancels the previous one.
  • Tell your coach if you think someone else has access to your account.

Availability and data

The app is run as a personal project. There is no guaranteed uptime, no support hours and no promise that a feature will keep working or stay available. It may change or stop without notice.

Your log is kept both on your device and in the database, and synchronised between them. That is reliable in normal use, but it is not a backup service. The app's settings include a backup you can copy and keep; use it before anything significant changes.

To the extent the law allows, the app is provided as it is, with no warranty, and neither injury arising from your training nor loss of data creates liability.

Ending it

You can stop at any time. Ask for your account and data to be deleted and it is done within 30 days — see the privacy notice. Your coach may close an account when the coaching relationship ends.

Applicable law

Finnish law applies. Your rights as a consumer under mandatory Finnish law are unaffected by anything written here.
